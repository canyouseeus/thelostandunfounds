01 · The Opportunity

A site built to bring you jobs.

ROA Industries has built its reputation in Texas on discipline, transparency, and a decade of hands-on craftsmanship. Your current Canva site tells that story, but it leaves money on the table — it doesn't qualify leads, doesn't follow up, and doesn't compound over time.

This proposal lays out a refreshed website built around one outcome: turning every visitor into a quote request. Modern, mobile-first, on the same Vercel + Supabase stack we run for THE LOST+UNFOUNDS.

  • A 9-field quote form — name, phone, service, budget, timeline, details — posted to a private database and your inbox.
  • Click-to-call everywhere — (956) 264-0888 in four places; roaindustriesllc@gmail.com in three.

Also included

  • Instant email notification on every new lead.
  • On-page SEO baseline — meta title, description, OpenGraph tags for clean social sharing.
  • 30 days of post-launch support.

How the 8% works · aligned incentives
Every quote-form lead is assigned a unique Lead ID. When ROA invoices that client through Stripe, the ID rides along in the payment metadata. On the 1st of each month, the dashboard sums all Stripe payments tagged to website leads and applies 8%. Walk-ins, existing clients, and non-website referrals are never included.
Timeline & Payment Terms
$500 due up front, in full, before work begins. Stripe invoice goes out the same day you approve. Site goes live within 7 days of payment clearing.
