What Stands Out

Search sends 13 people. Total.

Bing (6), Google (5), DuckDuckGo (1) and Brave (1) referred 13 visitors across nine months; 0.6% of traffic. Whatever the site is earning, it is not earning it in search results. That is the single largest untapped channel in this data.

Instagram is effectively the whole funnel

469 visitors arrived inside Instagram's in-app browser and 219 through its l.instagram.com redirector. With Facebook's 133, Meta properties account for nearly every referred visit. One platform's algorithm is the traffic strategy.

The December spike was the homepage

December ran 2,888 pageviews on 477 visitors; 6.1 pages each, the deepest month recorded. The homepage's 3,458 lifetime views is the only page large enough to have carried it; no single post or gallery is big enough to explain the peak.

Mac is 15% of visitors and 50% of pageviews

331 Mac visitors produced 4,875 pageviews; 14.7 each, against 3.3 on iOS. Desktop Safari alone shows 86 visitors against 2,170 views. That ratio is operator behaviour, not audience, and it inflates every site-wide average in this report.

/admin outranks /shop

/admin drew 263 visitors and 842 views; more than /shop (231 / 473). Much of that is the operator, but 263 uniques is high enough to be worth confirming the route's auth and RLS policies hold against anonymous probing.

Blog is the weakest section

The best-performing post, /blog/plutonium/to-maine-backand-back-again, drew 63 visitors and 66 views; a read-through of almost exactly one page. /thelostarchives index gets 167 visitors but 630 views, so the browsing happens on the index, not the posts.
