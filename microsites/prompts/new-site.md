# Prompt: validate a niche before building a microsite

**Run this before writing any content.** Most microsites fail at niche
selection, not execution. A ranked page in a worthless niche is a worse
outcome than no page — it consumed a domain, a build, and months of patience
to earn nothing.

---

## Prompt

> Assess whether `{{service}}` in `{{city}}, {{region}}` justifies a dedicated
> microsite. Answer each question separately and show your reasoning.
>
> 1. **Demand.** Realistic monthly search volume for the head term and its
>    close variants? Cite where the estimate comes from and state the
>    uncertainty. Say "unknown" rather than guessing.
> 2. **Competition.** Who currently ranks top 3 for the head term? For each:
>    directory, national aggregator, real local business, or thin affiliate
>    page? A page one of strong local businesses with mature Google Business
>    Profiles is a hard no.
> 3. **Lead value.** What is one converted customer worth? A niche where a
>    lead is worth under ~$50 cannot carry the cost of ranking it.
> 4. **Entity feasibility.** Can a *real* business be attached — a verifiable
>    Google Business Profile with a genuine address or service area, real
>    reviews, a phone a human answers? If not, stop here. A microsite with no
>    business entity behind it is largely invisible for local intent, and
>    faking one risks suspension.
> 5. **Differentiation.** What do we know or hold that a generic competitor
>    does not — real pricing, real photos, real local operating knowledge? If
>    the honest answer is "nothing", this is a content-spam build and should
>    not be made.
>
> Close with a recommendation: **build**, **build after X**, or **skip** — and
> if skip, say what would have to change.

---

## The bar

Build only on a **yes to 4** and a real answer to **5**. Everything else in
this pipeline is downstream of those two.
